Per-Coin Effects

Static % adds to the static group (with resistances, level and crits). Dynamic % adds to the dynamic group, which multiplies the static group. Multiplier × multiplies this coin’s final damage. Bonus % of Final Damage adds extra damage equal to a % of this coin’s damage. Flat Damage + adds a fixed amount. Atk Weight sets how many targets this coin hits (0 = use the Skill’s); tick Keep Weight for Next Coins to carry it over to the following coins.
